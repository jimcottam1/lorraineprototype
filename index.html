What is Reiki?

Reiki is a gentle, non-invasive healing practice that promotes balance and wellness. It can work alongside or as an alternative to traditional medical approaches, supporting your body's natural healing abilities.

Please note: Reiki is complementary to medical treatment and should never replace advice from your doctor.
