## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Consistent Branding
Your flyers and website now share the same golden color palette. Consider using #D4A056 (the golden color) for:
- Social media graphics
- Email signatures
- Future printed materials
- Business cards

### Tip 2: High-Quality Images
When you extract images from your PDFs:
- Use the highest resolution available
- Don't stretch or distort
- Keep aspect ratios
- Compress for web (use tinypng.com)

### Tip 3: Test Everything
After adding images:
- View on desktop
- View on mobile
- View on tablet
- Test in different browsers
- Ask a friend to check

### Tip 4: Keep Backups
Before making any changes:
- Save a copy of the entire folder
- Keep original images
- Take screenshots of what works
- Document any custom changes
